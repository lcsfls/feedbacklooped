Other

Share & embed

Link

Link to the form from your app, docs or emails. You can preset the category, a title and a context (e.g. the product and version), and pass the page the user came from.

Add &page=… with the current page URL from your app if you want to know where feedback came from. Submitters see it and can remove it.

Widget

A “Feedback” button for any website. It opens the form in a panel, including screenshots with markup and screen recording, and sends the page URL along (visible and removable for the user).

Only these sites may show the form in a frame; leave empty to switch the widget off. Save the settings before you test.

Paste it before </body>. If the site has a Content Security Policy, allow {{.BaseURL}} in script-src and frame-src. Use your own button instead with data-button="false" and Feedbacklooped.open().

Search engines & link previews

Only the feedback form, the roadmap and your imprint and privacy pages, with a sitemap.xml. Status pages, attachments and the admin area are always excluded. Off: everything is noindex.

The preview image is set below under Link preview image.

Spam protection

The visitor’s browser solves a small calculation in the background: no puzzle, no cookies, no third party. Requires JavaScript. Compatible with the ALTCHA protocol.

Times are averages on a current laptop; phones take about twice as long. Senders are told apart by a salted hash of their IP address that is kept in memory only.

Data protection

Cards in Done or Declined are deleted with all messages and attachments once they haven’t changed for this many days. Checked hourly.

Fonts, scripts and icons are always served from this server. Nothing is loaded from third parties, no cookies are set for visitors.
